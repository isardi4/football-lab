How metrics are calculated and compared
  • Historical minutes: sum of position intervals, excluding stoppage time; accounts for substitutions, dismissals and temporary absences. May differ from official minutes.
  • Per 90: total × 90 / minutes. Without minutes, the result is “—”; missing values are not converted to zero.
  • Goals: shots with a Goal outcome; excludes opponents’ own goals. This is the most directly comparable output dimension.
  • xG and xA: historical values from StatsBomb; xA is calculated from the xG of the shot linked to a pass. FPL uses its own provider. Showing them together does not imply identical models.
  • Assists: historical goal_assist versus fantasy assists. Shown separately without assuming equivalence.
  • Defence: tackles and recoveries follow each provider’s definitions. Historical CBI is reconstructed by adding Clearance, Block and Interception events. It approximates the FPL field rather than matching it exactly. Separate interceptions still have no modern counterpart.
  • Historical progression: completed passes advancing horizontally ≥12 StatsBomb units on a 120×80 pitch. This is the project’s own definition, not an official metric or complete tactical assessment.
  • Overall, Offensive and Defensive: each indicator uses lower ÷ higher × 100 on per-90 values. Offensive: goals, xG and xA. Defensive: recoveries, tackles and CBI; for goalkeepers: saves, goals conceded and clean sheets. Blocks average informative indicators and require at least two available, plus 900 minutes per player. Shared zeros provide no signal.
  • Overall weights: based on the original group: forwards 70% offensive / 30% defensive; midfielders 50% / 50%; defenders 30% / 70%; goalkeepers 0% / 100%. These are explicit project choices, not learned or validated weights. If a required block is missing, Overall cannot be assessed. Missing values are not replaced with zeros.
  • Goalkeepers: historical goals conceded are taken from the score when the goalkeeper played the full match. They are not attributed to outfield players. Saves are reconstructed by event type. The offensive component cannot be assessed without modern distribution data.
  • Candidates: up to seven are shown, ranked by the selected dimension. This is affinity across available indicators, not evidence of equal quality, complete tactical similarity or signing feasibility. Fantasy assists without a historical counterpart are excluded from the index.
  • Time windows: 2003/04 versus 2025/26 performance; the available squad is from 2026/27. New signings show their club during the measured window.
